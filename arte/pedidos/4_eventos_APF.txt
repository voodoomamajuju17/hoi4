On its remains, eight meganations wove something new, each around a single certainty: the forest, the machine, the contract, the sea, the sun, the harmony, the council or the emperor. In 2086, in Tillabéri, a schoolteacher gathered the village for six days and on the seventh they voted a water rota, written in chalk: the first council. A year later there were two hundred. In 2093, twelve hundred councils met in a stadium in Lagos and elected Amara. The Cape and the Ethiopian Highlands joined as members.\n\nWHAT IS AT STAKE\n\nAmara wants to integrate the members with full cores and ratify the Continental Constitution; commander Kwame Diallo wants to arm the councils and march to Suez and the Gulf. At the end waits The Original Human Empire.\n\nNEIGHBOURS AND RIVALS\n\nThe European Commune is the other collectivist answer: machines against councils. Rome demands tribute from us and contests the Mediterranean and the Desert Emirates. To the west, the Lawless Lands are our open frontier.\n\nThe nation's own mechanic is explained in the next event and in the How to Play decision.
