Shot straight-on from the front, soft studio light from the top left, everything in sharp focus. The badge carries ONE simple, bold emblem (at most two or three elements) that stands for the focus; its outline is one clear die-cut shape (round, shield, hexagon, gear, or the silhouette of the emblem itself). Badge centred, filling about 85% of the canvas, same camera, light and scale for the whole set. Isolated on a fully transparent background: no table, no backing card, no pin clasp visible, no cast shadow beyond a faint contact shadow. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.
